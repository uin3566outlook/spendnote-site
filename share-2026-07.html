EXPENSE TRACKER FOR IPHONE

Track your spending.
Keep it to yourself.

TrackMoney is a local-first expense tracker. There is no account, no ads, and no bank connection. Every record is stored on your iPhone — and only on your iPhone.

Coming to the App Store See the app
  • No account
  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • Works offline

On-device storage

Your ledger lives in a local database. There is no TrackMoney server, so there is nothing to breach and nothing to sell.

Log in seconds

Pick a category, tap the amount, done. No forms and no merchant fields — entry that holds up on busy days.

Gentle budget alerts

Heads-up notifications at 70%, 90% and 100% of a budget, automatically silenced between 10 pm and 8 am.

Face ID lock

Optionally lock the app with Face ID, so your numbers stay yours even when someone borrows your phone.

A LOOK INSIDE

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Three tabs cover the everyday work: this month at a glance, a searchable transaction list, and budgets that show their pace. Adding an expense is category, amount, done.

INSIGHTS · PRO

Understand where the money goes

Insights answers three questions about your month — what changed, why it moved, and what to do next. A monthly review closes the month with progress signals instead of a wall of transactions. All of it is computed on your iPhone from your own records.

  • Category breakdown with plain-language summaries
  • 3-, 6- and 12-month trends and budget forecasts
  • A calm monthly recap with shareable moments
  • Charts labeled with numbers, never color alone

SHARE CARDS

Share progress without sharing numbers

A good month is worth posting about. Your merchants, income and notes are not. Cards are attached to real conclusions from your month, and you pick whether they show exact amounts, rounded amounts, or percentages only.

Never on a card

  • Merchants
  • Income
  • Notes
  • Single transactions

WIDGETS · PRO

Your budget on the Home and Lock Screen

Widgets in three sizes read a local summary snapshot — remaining budget, spending and progress. The snapshot contains totals only, never a merchant or a transaction.

  • Home Screen widgets in three sizes
  • Lock Screen widget with budget progress
  • Reads on-device summary data only

YOUR DATA

Portable when you want it, gone when you say so

CSV export

Export your full history to CSV whenever you like. Free includes recent months; Pro unlocks the complete archive.

Local backup & restore

Create backups that stay in your files. Restores are validated before anything is replaced.

One currency, honestly

A single base currency per ledger, with no silent conversions of amounts you already recorded.

One-tap erase

Delete everything — records, budgets, widget snapshots and pending reminders — in a single action.
