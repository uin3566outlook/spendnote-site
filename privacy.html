Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how [Apple Developer Account Legal Name] (“TrackMoney,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the TrackMoney app.

1. Scope and provider

This policy applies to the TrackMoney iOS app and this website. The app is provided by [Apple Developer Account Legal Name]. For privacy questions, contact support@trackmoney.app.

This policy does not govern Apple’s handling of information through the App Store, Apple ID, or payment services. Those services are governed by Apple’s own policies.

2. Our local-first design

TrackMoney is designed to work without an account or a TrackMoney backend. In the current version, your ledger data remains on your device. TrackMoney does not transmit your ledger data to us, and we do not operate a service that receives or stores it.

TrackMoney does not include advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell your personal information, use it for cross-app advertising, or share it with data brokers.

3. Information stored on your device

To provide bookkeeping, budgeting, reminders, and personalization, the app stores the information you enter or choose locally on your device. This can include:

  • transaction and recurring-bill details, such as amounts, dates, categories, titles, merchants, and notes;
  • budget details, custom categories, region and currency selections;
  • app preferences, including Face ID lock and reminder settings;
  • locally generated CSV exports and backup archives while they are available to you; and
  • a limited aggregate snapshot in the TrackMoney App Group for the TrackMoney widget.

We use this information only on your device to provide the app’s requested features. We do not have access to it.

4. Device features

  • Face ID. If you enable Face ID lock, authentication is performed by iOS. TrackMoney receives only the authentication result and never receives or stores your biometric template.
  • Notifications. If you allow notifications, TrackMoney schedules local budget and review reminders through iOS. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings and turn reminders off in the app.
  • Widgets. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a local aggregate snapshot containing totals, budget status, currency, and related display state. They do not read transaction-level records, merchants, or notes.

5. Subscriptions and Apple

TrackMoney Pro subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through StoreKit and your App Store account. Apple controls pricing, billing, payment credentials, refunds, and subscription management. TrackMoney checks the StoreKit entitlement associated with the subscription products to unlock or restore Pro features. We do not receive or store your payment-card number, billing address, or Apple ID.

6. Exports and sharing

CSV exports, backup files, and share cards are created only when you choose to create them. If you use the iOS share sheet, Files, or another destination, you direct the selected content to that destination. The recipient’s or destination provider’s handling of the content is governed by its own privacy practices.

Share cards use rounded aggregate amounts by default. You may choose exact amounts or ratios. Merchants, income, notes, and individual transaction lines are not included in share cards.

7. Retention and deletion

TrackMoney retains your ledger data on your device until you delete it, restore another local backup over it, or remove the app in accordance with your device’s settings. In the app, Delete local data removes transactions, budgets, recurring bills, and custom categories, clears the widget ledger snapshot, and cancels queued budget reminders. Default categories may remain so the app can continue to function.

You control copies that you export, back up, or share. Delete those copies from their chosen location or recipient service separately. Because we do not receive your ledger data, we cannot retrieve or delete it from your device or from a destination that you chose.

8. Your privacy choices and rights

You can control your data directly in the app: edit or delete entries, export CSV data, create or restore a local backup, turn reminders on or off, disable Face ID lock, and decide whether to share a card. If applicable privacy law gives you additional rights, you may contact us using the details above. Our ability to act on a request is limited because we do not collect or retain your ledger data on our systems.

9. Children

TrackMoney is not directed to children. Do not use the app where you are below the minimum age required to consent to data processing under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us directly, contact us and we will address the request as required by applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices or applicable requirements change. If a future release adds iCloud sync, accounts, analytics, advertising, a backend, or third-party SDKs that handle personal data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before enabling those practices. The “Last updated” date shows when this policy was most recently revised.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact support@trackmoney.app.
