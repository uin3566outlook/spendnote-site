TRACKMONEY PRO

Subscription Terms

Last updated: July 16, 2026

TrackMoney Pro uses Apple App Store auto-renewable subscriptions. These terms explain what is billed by Apple and what Pro includes in the current version.

Products

TrackMoney Pro uses trackmoney.pro.monthly and trackmoney.pro.yearly. Displayed prices, trial availability, tax treatment, and billing currency are controlled by Apple in App Store Connect and may vary by storefront.

Billing and renewal

  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing period, following Apple's rules.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID account after any eligible free trial or introductory offer ends.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription in Apple account subscription settings.

Restore and refunds

Restore purchases uses Apple's restore flow and can re-check subscription entitlement on this device. Refund requests, billing disputes, and some cancellation outcomes are handled by Apple under Apple's policies.

What Pro includes today

Pro includes deeper spending insights, longer trend views, monthly review, unlimited budgets, privacy-safe Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and full-history CSV export. iCloud sync and shared ledger are not current delivered subscription benefits.

Contact

Subscription questions can be sent to support@trackmoney.app.
