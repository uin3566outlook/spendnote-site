Support

For help with TrackMoney, contact us by email. We do not provide support through social networks, and this page does not collect form submissions.

Contact

support@trackmoney.app

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, TrackMoney app version, and a description of the issue. Do not include transaction notes, merchants, CSV files, or other sensitive financial information unless we explicitly ask for it.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. Use Restore Purchases in TrackMoney after reinstalling or switching devices, and manage cancellation through Apple account subscription settings. Refunds and billing disputes are handled by Apple.

Privacy and local data

TrackMoney is local-first. You can export or delete local data from Settings. Read the Privacy Policy for the current data-handling description.
