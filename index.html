EXPENSE TRACKER FOR IPHONE

Track your spending.
Keep it to yourself.

SpendNote is a local-first expense tracker. There is no account, no ads, and no bank connection. Every record is stored on your iPhone — and only on your iPhone.

  • No account

On-device storage

Your ledger lives in a local database. There is no SpendNote server, so there is nothing to breach and nothing to sell.

Log in seconds

Pick a category, tap the amount, done. No forms and no merchant fields — entry that holds up on busy days.

Gentle budget alerts

Heads-up notifications at 70%, 90% and 100% of a budget, automatically silenced between 10 pm and 8 am.

Face ID lock

Optionally lock the app with Face ID, so your numbers stay yours even when someone borrows your phone.

A LOOK INSIDE

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Home, transactions, budgets, and settings keep everyday money tasks within easy reach, with a centered quick-add action when you need to log something fast.

INSIGHTS · FREE + PRO

Understand where the money goes

Free includes a current-month snapshot. Pro adds category drivers, context, and longer-term trends. Everything is computed on your iPhone from your own data.

  • Current-month snapshot with budget context
  • Category breakdown and plain-language drivers
  • Monthly trends with exact values and labels
  • Advanced reports remain on device

MONTHLY REVIEW · PRO

See what changed, not just what you spent

Monthly Review compares the current view with the previous period, then shows which categories moved the total. Narrow the result by date, type, category, or note when you need a precise answer.

  • Period-over-period spending comparison
  • Category changes with exact amounts
  • Flexible date, type, category, and note filters
  • Export the transactions matching the current view

RECURRING CONTROL · PRO

Know what is committed before the month begins

Recurring Control brings fixed commitments into one view, from monthly and annual totals to upcoming costs and category owners.

  • Monthly and annual committed totals
  • Category shares across recurring costs
  • Upcoming charges and subscription totals
  • All calculations stay on device

YOUR DATA

Portable when you want it, gone when you say so

CSV export

Export your full history to CSV whenever you like. Free includes recent months; Pro unlocks the complete archive.

Local backup & restore

Create backups that stay in your files. Restores are validated before anything is replaced.

One currency, honestly

A single base currency per ledger, with no silent conversions of amounts you already recorded.

One-tap erase

Delete everything — records, budgets, widget snapshots and pending reminders — in a single action.
